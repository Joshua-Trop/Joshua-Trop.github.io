About

Hey guys!! My name is Josh and welcome to my portfolio website! At the time of creating this website I am a senior at Columbia High School; in addition I am also a second year student in Gaming and Multimedia at Robert H. Gibson Technical School. I'm obsessed with Portal (and Portal 2) and Stardew Valley. The latter game has greatly influenced any game projects I've worked on.

Writing Experience

I currently am writing the story for my most recent game project "Harpoon Bird Fishing" (you can read more about that in the "Portfolio Section). I also play DnD which requires a lot of on-the-go story writing and I am currently working on a fan-made Zelda-themed TTRPG (Table Top Roleplaying Game). Lastly I've also written a few short stories, mainly fan stuff such as "Minecraft" books and some "Wings of Fire" fan fics.

Audio Experience

My favorite form of audio work is voice acting, this is something I practice with my grandfather who does voice over work for audio books. I have one role that I've voice acted for, I played a character named 'Krow' in my friend's game "Operation White Wave." I like doing crazy character voices, but this character had a much deeper voice which I also really enjoyed doing. In addition I've also made some music using an online web step-sequencer called "Song Maker."
