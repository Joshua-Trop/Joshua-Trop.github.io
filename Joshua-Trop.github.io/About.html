Schooling Background

As of developing this website I'm a senior at Columbia High School. I'm also enrolled at Robert H. Gibson Technical School as a second year Gaming and Multimedia student. It is in the Gaming and Multimedia class that I learned the majority of the skills I'm presenting in this website. This program helped me develop my writing skills as well as my voice acting, SFX design, and music development skills.

Hobbies

One of my favorite hobbies is cosplaying, I'll do almost any kind of cosplaying if I'm being honest, the majority of my cosplays are Star Wars cosplays. I make most of my cosplays through 3D printing, in addition I have bought a Link (Zelda BOTW/TOTK) cosplay and a custom Mandalorian cosplay. I also enjoy playing DnD, I'm a casual dungeon master. Lastly I love collecting Legos, yes it's an addiction and yes I can stop whenever I'd like.

Why I Do What I Do

I was inspired to write stories for videogames through the "Horizon" franchise as well as "The Last of Us." I thought both of these series had beautiful stories that got me quite close to crying at some points and I decided that I'd really like to be a part of the team that creates stories as beautiful as that. In addition I'm a teenager and one of the traits that comes with that is self expression which I get to do a lot of through my writing and voice acting. Whether it's expressing my own thoughts, opinions, or feelings I always get to have fun and showcase interesting ideas.
