Music Examples

"Project 1"

As the name suggests this was the first song I created using 'Song Maker'. This was made for an assignment in my Gaming and Multimedia class. It's simple and focuses on a standard bass line and simple chord.

"Deeper Voice"

This was the second song I made using 'Song Maker'. I wanted to create a song with a bass drop thus "Deeper Voice" was born. It focuses on a low bass part and simple melodic notes.

"Not a Standard Youtube Intro"

My third and favorite creation, "Not a Standard Youtube Intro" has been commonly referred to as "sounding like a Youtube intro" thus it's name was born out of irony. It was very experimental and foucsses on the beat and melody.

Game Design Projects

"Harpoon Bird Fishing Tutorial Script"

"Harpoon Bird Fishing" is a game still in the beta phase that I developed during my senior year in high school. I worked with four other students in my class, under the name 'ManaForge,' to develop this project. I mainly focused on creating all the layouts. I also wrote the dialgue and did a decent amount of coding, specifically for interacting with objects and NPCs.

"Under the Strip"

"Under the Strip" is a game I helped develop during my junior year in high school. It still has a lot of unfinished assets as we haven't fully completed developing the game. I worked with three other students, under the name 'ManaForge,' to create this game. I focused mainly on SFX designs for this project, using platforms like 'Reaper' to mix audio to create the ambience and feedback sounds you hear in the game.
